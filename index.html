About

About Me

My academic background spans theoretical physics, probability, mathematical finance, numerical methods, and machine learning. I enjoy problems that require both mathematical understanding and implementation, especially when theory can be tested through simulation or computational experiments.

My current work centers on dependence modeling with copulas and simulation of dependent random variables. Alongside this research, I work on numerical computing and machine-learning projects, including experiments with GPT-style models and efficient simulation methods.

Outside research, I compose music and play piano in big bands. I am interested in building a career where mathematical reasoning, computation, and real-world decision making meet.

Research

Research Interests

Theory → Simulation → Implementation

Copulas & Dependence Modeling

Modeling multivariate dependence structures beyond linear correlation, with attention to tail behavior and asymmetric dependence.

  • Sklar's theorem
  • Elliptical and Archimedean copulas
  • Tail dependence
  • High-dimensional dependence

Dependent Random Number Generation

Developing scalable simulation methods for dependent random variables and studying their efficiency in Monte Carlo applications.

  • Rejection sampling
  • Conditional distribution methods
  • Monte Carlo simulation
  • Numerical validation

Mathematical Finance

Applying probability, stochastic analysis, and numerical methods to pricing, risk evaluation, and quantitative decision making.

  • Stochastic processes
  • Risk-neutral pricing
  • Deep hedging
  • Simulation-based valuation
